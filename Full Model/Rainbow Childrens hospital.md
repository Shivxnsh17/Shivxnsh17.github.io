# Rainbow Children's Hospital — Full Financial Model

A comprehensive, multi-sheet financial model built for Rainbow Children's Medicare Limited, covering three-statement forecasting and full equity valuation.

**Key Areas Covered:**
- Three-statement model: Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement
- Revenue and expense forecasting
- Valuation: DCF and comparable company analysis
- Key operating and financial metrics
